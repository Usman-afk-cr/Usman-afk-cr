Areas of work include:

## Research & Predictive Modeling

### Aircraft Engine Remaining Useful Life (RUL) Prediction

**Focus:** Predictive Maintenance · Machine Learning · Time Series / Sequential Data

Worked on an aircraft engine Remaining Useful Life prediction project and recently wrote a research paper associated with this work.

The broader objective of RUL prediction is to estimate how much useful operating life remains before maintenance may be required.

Key areas of interest:

- Predictive maintenance and condition-based monitoring.
- Machine learning for engine degradation modeling.
- Remaining Useful Life estimation.
- Model evaluation and error analysis.
- Research-driven experimentation and technical documentation.

**Research paper:** Written; publication status and paper details can be added once finalized.
